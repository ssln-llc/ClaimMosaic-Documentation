The **Files** tab contains the documents, images, and other files associated with a claim. Use it to upload and organize files, create selected downloads, and share files outside Claim Mosaic.

### File and Folder Management

- **Upload Files:** Upload one or more files by dragging them onto the page or clicking **Upload Files**.
- **Create Folders:** Keep your documents organized by creating folders. Click the **New Folder** button to add a new folder to the claim.
- **Choose a View:** Switch between **Grid** and **List** view in the Files toolbar.
- **Sort Files:** Sort by **Name** or **Date Uploaded**, then use the direction control to switch between ascending and descending order.
- **Preview Files:** Click a file to open its preview. Images and supported media open in the gallery.
- **View Details:** Use **View Details** on a file or folder to see its name, description, creator, dates, and size. File details also show the current folder. From the details panel, you can rename the item, edit its description, download it, or delete it.
- **Move Files Between Folders:** Open a file's details and use the **Folder** field to move it to another folder or back to **Main Files**. From Main Files, you can also drag a file onto a folder.
- **Download All Files:** Use **Download All Files** in the toolbar to export the claim's files as a ZIP file.

### Select, download, and share files

1. Click **Select Items**.
2. Select individual files or folders. You can also click **Select all** for the items currently shown. Your selection remains active while you open folders and add more files.
3. Click **Download** to download the selected files as a ZIP. Selecting a folder includes the files inside it.
4. To share the selection, click **Share Link**. This action is available to users who can update the claim.
5. In **Create Share Link**, optionally add an expiration date, password, or note.
6. Click **Create Link**, then click **Copy** beside the generated link.

### Photo Report (Claims)

Open **Generate** in the claim header and select **Photo Report**.

1. Select the photos to include.
2. Choose **1**, **2**, or **4** photos per page.
3. Click **Generate Report**.
4. Keep the window open to watch progress, or close it while Claim Mosaic continues processing. When the email option is available, you can request a download link that expires after 24 hours.
5. When the report is ready, review the preview and click **Download**.

### Document Generation

:::note[Feature Availability]
Document Generation is available on the **Professional** and **Enterprise** tiers.
:::

Use **Document from Template** to create a document from a pre-configured template, such as a Letter of Representation (LOR).

1. Open **Generate** in the claim header and select **Document from Template**.
2. Select a template from the sidebar.
3. Review the template sections. If the template contains required PDF sections, upload a PDF for each one.
4. Claim Mosaic merges the current claim's data, such as the policyholder name and policy number, into the template.
5. Click **Preview** to inspect the result or **Download** to save the generated document.

### E-Signatures

:::note[Feature Availability]
E-Signatures are available on the **Enterprise** tier.
:::

Templates that require signatures include a **Signature** section in the document viewer.

1. Open **Generate**, select **Document from Template**, and choose a template that requires signatures.
2. In the **Signature** section, select the contacts who need to sign.
3. To require signatures in a specific order, enable **Request Signatures in Order** and arrange the recipients.
4. Click **Send for Signature**.

The selected contacts will receive an email with a secure link to review and sign the document electronically. The signed document will be automatically saved back to the claim's files.

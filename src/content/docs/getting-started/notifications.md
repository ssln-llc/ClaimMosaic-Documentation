Select the **bell** in the top bar to open the **Notification center**. Mentions, task activity, and completed reports appear here.

## Manage Your Notifications

Select a notification to read it and, when available, open the related work. Use **Mark read** or **Mark unread** to keep track of what needs attention. To be reminded later, select **Snooze**, choose a time, and confirm with **Snooze**. Select **Dismiss** when you are done. The **Active**, **Snoozed**, and **Dismissed** tabs help you find each group again.

## Enable Desktop Notifications

Desktop notifications alert you when someone mentions you, even if Claim Mosaic is not open in a browser tab. To turn them on:

1. Open the user menu in the top-right corner and select **Profile**.
2. Find **Desktop Notifications** and select **Enable notifications**.
3. Allow notifications when your browser asks.

This setting applies to the browser you use to enable it. Repeat the steps in your other browsers. If notifications are blocked, allow them for Claim Mosaic in your browser's site settings, then select **Check again** on your profile. The Desktop Notifications section appears only in supported browsers.

Your profile also has separate settings for the daily digest and email notifications when someone tags you.
